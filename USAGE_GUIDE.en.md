# NWC_PUBLIC — Public Usage Guide

## What is allowed without separate approval
Viewing, downloading, learning, non-production technical evaluation by individuals or companies, local testing, and evaluation-only modifications.

## Prior written approval required
Actual internal business processing, customer implementation/delivery, production services, commercial integrations, resale and independent redistribution.

GitHub-hosted public repository forks are governed by GitHub's terms; they do not confer production or external redistribution rights.

## Authorization requests
Identify legal entity, contact, project, dev/test/production environment, target features and version, distribution audience, redistribution intent, duration and purpose.

## Publication rules
Publish approved files as independent release commits (never full NWC_DEV history). Check secrets, third-party licenses, dependencies, sourcemaps, and links before release. External pull requests are subject to `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
